## What's launching

The We Are Distributed job board, at <https://www.wearedistributed.org/jobs>. Every job is sorted into open to you, remote but not from your location, or on-site somewhere else, and marked remote, hybrid or on-site. Only roles posted in the last 14 days make the board. The Job Tracker and the Resume Analyzer are the second act.

## Rules for this project

- Product Hunt forbids asking for votes. Nothing in this project asks for upvotes, in any wording.
- We Are Distributed submits the launch and replies to comments on the day. Those aren't tasks.
