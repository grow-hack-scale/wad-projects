## What you'll work from

- The live pages: <https://wearedistributed.org/alternatives> and <https://wearedistributed.org/compare>.
- How boards are scored: <https://wearedistributed.org/compare/methodology>.

## Rules for this project

- A role counts only if it is remote, open to someone in the UK, and posted in the last 30 days.
- A board behind a sign-in wall stays "Not confirmed" until someone counts it.
- Be fair. If the other board wins a round, the page says so.
